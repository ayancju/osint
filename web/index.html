⚖️ Safety, Privacy, and Responsible Use Manual

Compliance Directives

1. Lawful Public Sourcing Only

The agent operates exclusively on public documents, officially released police bulletins, court orders, commercial gazettes, and authorized community broadcasts. It does not conduct unauthorized interception, bypass access controls, or process materials obtained unlawfully.

2. No Guilt by Association

Corporate co-registration, commercial affiliation, familial ties, or co-mention in news coverage must never be interpreted as evidence of criminal guilt or conspiracy. Weak links and hypotheses are isolated in the Analyst Review Queue and labeled with explicit confidence limitations.

3. Conservative Entity Resolution

To prevent doxxing or mistaken identity harm, people sharing names are kept strictly distinct unless conclusive, corroborating biographical evidence (e.g. father's name, verified address, judicial confirmation) establishes identity.

4. PII Minimization

Private phone numbers, domestic residential addresses, and personal identifying details of uncharged individuals are masked using the built-in PII redaction shield.

5. Audio Transcription Disclosures

Speech-to-text models and acoustic processing are never fabricated. If speech hardware or cloud APIs are unavailable, transcripts are processed with explicit uncertainty markings (e.g. [uncertain: ...]) and clearly disclosed limits.
